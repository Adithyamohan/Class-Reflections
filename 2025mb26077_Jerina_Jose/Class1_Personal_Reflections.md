**Personal Reflections Session 1**



* AI is used to build products. Embed AI into a product (Customers experience it in the way we expose it)
* AI can predict problems and help us take action before they become bigger issues.
* AI can help reduce repetitive work and save time in daily activities.



* Product Managers act as CEO of the product and are accountable not just for building an AI solution, but for both success and failure.
* Product Managers define product vision, strategy, and have a responsibility to ensure that the product delivers the intended outcome and creates real value for its users.
* A traditional PM is often responsible for deciding what should be built, why it should be built, and coordinating teams to get it delivered.
* Product Managers' work is not just about planning; it involves prioritising requirements, coordinating with different teams, solving issues, reviewing progress, and making decisions to keep the product moving toward its goals.
* The PM skill stack showed me that product management requires a mix of customer and business judgement, communication, problem-solving, prioritisation, and technical awareness.



* I realised product management is challenging because a PM has to balance different stakeholder expectations, limited resources, changing priorities, and user needs while still being accountable for the outcome.
* AI should be used as a supporting tool for people, while human knowledge and experience remain important for making the final decision.
